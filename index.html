📖 Quick Health Glossary
Allergies
Foods or substances your body reacts badly to — causing rashes, itching, swelling, stomach upset, or breathing problems. Common: peanuts, milk (lactose), gluten, eggs, fish, soy, alcohol.
Chronic Conditions
Long-term health issues you live with. Examples: Diabetes (blood sugar problems), Hypertension (high blood pressure), Asthma (breathing condition), Kidney disease.
Active Medications
Drugs you currently take. SafeScan checks if a scanned product interacts with them. Include the dose if you know it, e.g. "Aspirin 100mg".
Dietary Preferences
Food choices you follow — by personal choice, religion or health. Examples: Vegan (no animal products), Vegetarian, Halal, Kosher, Gluten-free, Lactose-free, Sugar-free. SafeScan flags products that don't match.
Why this matters
The more SafeScan knows about your body, the better it can tell you whether a product is right for you specifically — not just people in general.
